I'm a researcher and software engineer. I build AI systems and the evaluations that show whether they work, mostly around better retrieval for LLMs and the precision limits of computer vision. Oh, … and I row!

Research

My doctoral research with Prof. Thomas Bohné at the Cyber-Human Lab, University of Cambridge, was on the use of computer vision and camera-based tracking to capture and measure the human body. My thesis focused on the causes of accuracy loss in markerless pose estimation, and on how to tell when a measurement should not be trusted. It was funded by an EPSRC iCASE studentship with Microsoft Research, and submitted in 2026.

Engineering

I'm a computer scientist by training and have worked across the stack, from low-level systems to large-scale data processing, with most of my work on data and the pipelines around it. I've worked in corporate software, as an early hire and later in technical leadership at start-ups, as founding engineer and technical founder, and as a freelancer. My recent work has been research engineering, taking prototypes through to deployment. I'm now exploring my own start-up, Volent, which builds high-recall retrieval for large language models. Some of my projects are on GitHub.

Rowing

I learnt to row at Cambridge and joined the German national team in 2018. With my crews I won Paralympic bronze in the PR3 mixed double sculls at Paris 2024, and medals at World and European Championships. More on rowing.
